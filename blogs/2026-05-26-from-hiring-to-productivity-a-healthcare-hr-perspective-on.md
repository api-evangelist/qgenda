Healthcare organizations invest significant time and resources into recruiting and hiring. But between offer acceptance and day one on the job, there’s a critical phase that often determines whether that investment pays off: Readiness. Readiness is the bridge between hiring and productivity – and one of the most underestimated pressure points in workforce management.
