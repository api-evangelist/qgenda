Scheduling — Where Workforce Plans Become Reality Healthcare organizations spend significant time planning their workforce – hiring, onboarding, and preparing staff to meet patient demand. But it’s scheduling where those plans become real. Scheduling determines who is working, when they’re working, and where care happens.
